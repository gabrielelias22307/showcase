Object facts

Maker
David Drake ("Dave"), about 1801 to the 1870s
Date
May 3, 1862
Place
Lewis Miles' pottery, Edgefield District, South Carolina
Material
Stoneware clay with an alkaline glaze
Held by
National Museum of American History, Smithsonian
Marks
"May 3, 1862 / LM Dave"

Dave scratched poems into about twenty surviving jars. In 1834, South Carolina made it a crime to teach enslaved people to read or write.

"I made this jar all of cross
If you don't repent, you will be lost"
