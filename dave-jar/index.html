Jar Made by "Dave"

Object facts

Maker
David Drake ("Dave"), about 1801 to the 1870s
Date
May 3, 1862
Place
Lewis Miles' pottery, Edgefield District, South Carolina
Material
Stoneware clay with an alkaline glaze
Held by
National Museum of American History, Smithsonian
Marks
"May 3, 1862 / LM Dave" on the other side

Dave was enslaved for most of his life. He made thousands of huge storage jars for the pottery. On about twenty surviving jars he scratched short poems into the wet clay. That was dangerous: an 1834 South Carolina law made it a crime to teach enslaved people to read or write.

"I made this jar all of cross
If you don't repent, you will be lost"
